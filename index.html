    HAPPIEST 17TH BIRTHDAY, BABY! ❤️

    Sometimes I still think about how a simple Instagram Note brought you into my life. I was the one who approached you first, and honestly, that was one of the best decisions I’ve ever made. I never expected that one little message would turn into late-night talks, endless laughter, and a relationship that means so much to me. I’m very thankful that I met a pretty girl like you, baby, THE PRETTIEST GIRL EVER, and I’m so lucky that I get to call you mine. ❤️

    I love all the little things we do together, from watching movies and playing games to our random conversations and late-night talks about our future. Even though I’m not really a fan of horror movies and games, I still enjoy watching and playing them with you, even if I’m secretly scared sometimes HAHAHA. It’s the little moments like these that make me realize how much I love spending time with you.

    I’m also very thankful for how you always try to understand me, especially when things don’t go the way we want them to. Thank you for always finding ways to fix things with me instead of letting our problems come between us. I know we aren’t perfect, and there will always be times when we misunderstand each other, but I’m happy that we keep trying and choosing to work things out together. It means so much to me that we can learn from our mistakes and grow together. I hope we continue to understand each other and choose each other even when things get difficult.

    I’m thankful, too, because you always find ways to make me happy. You never let me feel bad about myself, and you always remind me of the good things in me through your sweet compliments. You probably don’t realize how much those little words mean to me, but they really do, baby. Sometimes, when I’m not feeling good about myself, the way you reassure me makes me feel so much better. Thank you for making me feel appreciated and loved, and for reminding me that I’m someone worth believing in. I’m so lucky to have someone as loving and thoughtful as you.

    One of my favorite things about us is praying together at night. It makes me happy knowing that we get to share that moment before ending our day, thanking God and praying for the things we hope for. Sometimes I end up falling asleep while we’re praying😓😓, but please know that it doesn’t make those moments any less special to me. Praying with you always makes me happy, and I’m thankful that we can include God in our relationship. I hope we continue praying together and asking Him to guide us as we grow and build our future.

    Baby, I honestly can’t wait to finally be with you and not be in a long-distance relationship anymore, hehe. I can’t wait for the day when I can hug you whenever I want, hold your hand while we walk together, watch movies beside you, and spend time with you without having to rely on our phones. I want to experience all those little things we’ve been talking about and turn our dreams into real memories. I know we still have to be patient, but I’m looking forward to the day when the distance is no longer between us. I hope that, in God’s perfect time, we get to celebrate your birthdays together and make so many more memories in person. ❤️

    On your special day, I pray that God blesses you with good health, happiness, peace of mind, and success in everything you do. I hope all your dreams come true and that you always find reasons to smile, even on difficult days. Always remember that I’m proud of you, baby, and I’ll always support you in whatever you want to achieve. You don’t have to have everything figured out right away. Take your time, enjoy being 17, and never forget how special you are.

    Thank you for being part of my life, baby. Thank you for the laughter, the conversations, the prayers, the compliments, and all the little things you do that make my days better. Thank you for being patient with me, for trying to understand me, and for always making an effort to make things right between us. I’m so happy that I met you, and if I could go back to that Instagram Note, I’d still choose to approach you all over again because it led me to the prettiest girl ever and someone who means so much to me.

    Happy 17th birthday again, baby! I hope this new chapter brings you beautiful experiences, new opportunities, and countless reasons to smile. No matter what life brings, I hope we continue choosing each other, growing together, and building the future we always talk about.

    I LOVE YOU SO MUCH, BABY! I’m so thankful that I have you in my life, and I’m excited for all the memories we have yet to make. I can’t wait to be with you someday and finally give you all the hugs and kisses I’ve been saving. HEHE. Happy birthday, my pretty girl! ❤️❤️❤️

    Always yours,
    John Dominique G. Boyles
