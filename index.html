    My dearest Wyn Keysha,

    Happy birthday, my love. I keep thinking about how a simple Instagram Note brought you into my life. I was the one who approached you first — best decision I ever made, even if I didn't know it yet. Somehow, a little message turned into late-night talks I never wanted to end.

    I love the life we're building in the small moments: watching movies together, playing multiplayer games, and me pretending I'm not scared during the horror parts. You like horror movies and horror games. I don't. But I still watch and play them with you, because time with you matters more to me than avoiding a jump scare. (I'm still watching through my fingers, by the way.)

    Somehow you made me the guy who says "okay, put on another one" while secretly checking behind him. That's love, I think.

    Some of my favorite memories are the ones that happen late at night, when we talk about our future and imagine what it would be like to finally be together. I love laughing with you about random things, and I love that we can be completely ourselves with each other.

    I also love that we don't pretend we're perfect. We've had hard moments. We've talked things through, tried to understand each other, and chosen to communicate instead of walking away. Those conversations mean a lot to me. They remind me that we're not just here for the easy days — we're choosing to grow together.

    And I will never be normal about our video calls. When brain fog hits and your eyes do that little split-apart thing, I laugh every single time. Not at you — it's just one of those tiny, ridiculous, very "us" moments I love being part of.

    On your birthday, I hope you feel exactly how much you're loved. I hope this year brings you peace, good health, success, and a hundred reasons to smile. I hope you get to rest when you're tired, laugh until your cheeks hurt, and feel supported on the days that aren't so easy.

    I hope we keep making memories — the silly ones, the quiet ones, and the ones we'll talk about for years. I don't need a perfect story. I just want our story, with all its laughter and honesty and the way we keep choosing each other.

    Happy birthday, my favorite person. Thank you for being you.

    Always yours,
    John Dominique G. Boyles
